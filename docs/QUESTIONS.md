## Introduction & Citizenship

**Q1**: What do you pledge at the citizenship ceremony?
**A**: You pledge to be loyal to Australia and its people, respect their rights and liberties, and uphold and obey its laws.

**Q2**: Who can deliver a Welcome to Country?
**A**: A recognized Aboriginal elder or traditional custodian of the land. It is performed to welcome visitors to their traditional and ancestral lands.

**Q3**: Who can deliver an Acknowledgement of Country?
**A**: Anyone, whether Indigenous or non-Indigenous, can deliver an Acknowledgement of Country.

**Q4**: What does an Acknowledgement of Country recognize?
**A**: An Acknowledgement of Country recognizes that the gathering is on Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander land.

**Q5**: Who is Australia's current Head of State?
**A**: His Majesty King Charles III is Australia's current Head of State.
